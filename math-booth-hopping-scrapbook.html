MATH BOOTH HOPPING

THE REFLECTION

How it all added up

During the math booth hopping activity, I loved how creative everything was — from the human calculator (literally), to the shooting game, to the magic tricks. It all came together like graphs of different functions overlapping to form one big graph art piece.

But the creativity didn't stop there — each booth also tried to bend your mind, whether through geometry puzzles, an equation-based memory game (I lost because my opponent was faster, but it was still exhilarating and mind-bending), or physics questions that made you actually visualize what was happening, kind of like how Einstein imagined riding alongside a beam of light.

Through it all, it reminded me that math, science, and creativity aren't separate variables — they're all part of the same language the universe speaks. Honestly, it was just really fun. That's all there is to it.

Job Cedrick M. Hamile
